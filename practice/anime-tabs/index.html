Anime tab studies

The anime section now holds 43 anime and 16 films, split into past · 2026 · 2027. It has to sort two ways at once: when you watched something, and what it is. Each option below answers that differently. The frames are your real site; scroll and click inside them. For now everything is in past, so 2026 and 2027 show “nothing here yet.”

A · Index chosen tabStyle: "index"

Idea
The years are large serif tabs with counts, like chapter headings: past⁵⁹ 2026⁰ 2027⁰. One year at a time. A quiet all · anime · films row underneath.
Why yours
It's the only option that answers both questions. Merging films into anime makes that second filter worth having. The serif matches your section titles, so the years read as part of the page, not as controls.
Risk
Two rows of controls instead of one, and it looks different from projects. Large tabs use more height before the posters start.

B · Marks runner-up tabStyle: "marks"

Idea
The same radio row as projects: ○ ALL ○ past ○ 2026 ○ 2027. (When studied, films carried a small ( film ) tag under the title; those tags were later removed, so with this style anime and films would look alike.)
Why yours
It's what you pointed at. One system across the site: once a visitor learns projects, they know anime. The quietest option, so the posters do the talking.
Risk
It only filters by year. Anime and films are told apart by a tiny tag, and with ALL first, the 16 films sit at the very bottom of 59 posters.

C · Shelves tabStyle: "shelves"

Idea
Nothing is hidden. Each year is a shelf with its own heading and count, stacked down the page. The row at the top turns into jump links: past ↓ 2026 ↓ 2027 ↓.
Why yours
It reads like a timeline of your watching, and a visitor never misses anything behind a tab. Best once every year has a good number of entries.
Risk
Right now it's one 59-poster shelf with two empty headings far below it. Long page, and no anime/film split.
