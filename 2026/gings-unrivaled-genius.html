Ging's Unrivaled Genius

Ging changed his friends name from this:

Wdwune

To this:

Dwun

Just to fit the first letter of his name into the name of the game that he thought of. GREED ISLAND.

Ging recruits Razor, a convicted felon, to enjoy the game that he’s created. “It was like talking to a little kid rambling about a game that he created.” Yet, Ging was not afraid to let Razor show his true strength in front of Gon when he came to clear the game. In fact, he has full confidence in his own abilities as a hunter when he says to Razor, “don’t get cocky. He’s my son.” It goes to show the lengths he went to recruit Razor and the trust he has in the bonds he’s formed.

Ging apprenticed Kite. He threw him into the deep end with his final test. Find me. This led Kite to Whale Island and the detour of meeting Gon - giving him Ging’s license so that when he grows older it serves as a testament to him having become a full fledged hunter, having found his dad. All good hunters are loved by animals.

Ging likes to have fun. During the thirteenth chairman election arc, Ging came days earlier than everyone else and predicted what would happen. He left before anyone knew. He threw Cheadle off her composure, he left Pariston in the dust, and he met Gon’s friend, a certain bespectacled pervert, a loyal friend, and an amazing human being with immense potential during a chance encounter in his detoured pursuit that is running for the chairman of the hunter association - he didn’t care much for the position, but he did care that Netero’s will was carried out. And that means, not letting Pariston do whatever he pleases, corrupting the association, but rather letting things pan out in the way that ensures the transferring of authority goes smoothly. In any case, he still quit the Zodiacs in pursuit of unrestrained exploration of the Dark Continent.

A - a man devoted wholly to his interests; his stream of consciousness thinking is reflected through Togashi’s narrative - dynamics of switching between light and dark tones for every arc, progressing in maturity - for the themes tackled in Zoldyck family arc, there is a light-heartedness to training and strengthening relationships in lighter arcs, then thrown into the deep end in Yorknew City, and once again building on the intricate worldbuilding, exploring the complexities of Nen, and the dynamics between characters in Greed Island, the story matures even more in Chimera Ant, with the thirteenth chairman election arc new characters are introduced and primed with the structure of the hunter association, and now entering Succession War Arc

B - obsessed with exploration and detours; often he is very messy - his hair, his clothes, his room is filled with useless junk, he likes collecting things as a hobby, walks how he wants to walk, recruits members to get things done and relies on them to accomplish the vision that he had in mind from the beginning, whether it be a card game, a notepad placed in a meeting room, a cassette tape and a box, the view from the world tree, none of it would have been possible without the intense self-efficacy to push frontiers of uncharted territory, and the level of trust his peers had in the vision and their work to succeed despite the unknown, uncertainty, complexities, and failures to arrive at the intended result

C - caught in a high stakes battle between two values - either joining the team, and leave behind his position as number 2, or leave the team - Ging thinks of a third alternative, that works better for both sides, call it bribery, or insensitivity to the loyalty and brand of Beyond Netero’s expedition team, everyone on the expedition team is crazy talented, has devoted their time, sacrificed obligations to come here, why should they submit to the order of a newcomer who just came in and demands to be number 2, he acknowledges that there may be concerns regarding trust, and address each individual concern accordingly, be it money, dominating Usamen in his self-proclaimed mastery of the game Pip-Play, beating Curly to several ancient linguistics word games, and overall enjoying his time before they get there, getting to know the members of the group, getting prepared and comfortable in knowing people’s quirks and communication styles, to get his objective of solidifying his command as number 2 of the expedition team

D - this third or sometimes even fourth or fifth alternative, is often used by Ging to create situations more favorable than the normal or conventional thinking, and he knowingly calculates and interprets the unintended risks and consequences of each option, before his target hones in on his motives. In this sense, Ging is often seen four or five steps ahead of every other player during the chairman election arc. He understands what each player wants to get out of the meeting, he knows what Cheadle desires, he knows what Pariston desires, and he analyzes what would be most interesting for him to see. With that in mind, it’s not hard to see that Ging is often mischaracterized - some say he is this, others differ in opinion as to who he is - and this is due to the fact that Ging does not appraise things based on some inherent or base metric, formula, or ideology. He is a maverick, an unorthodox and independent-minded person. So labels like the top five Nen users in the world, triple-star hunter, mentor, a messy, unsociable, cocky brat, a capable, talented, but annoying friend, a selfish prick, a terrible dad, are just, as the name suggests, labels. He is well loved by animals, he obtains results when they are high priority to him, and when it comes down to it, he does care about people. That said, he’s not sociable at all when put in the same room as the Zodiacs, but in terms of intellect and processing aptitude, he is in a class of his own. During the elections arc, we see Beans ask him why he did something, and Cheadle asks him for advice, albeit begrudgingly, because they want to know how a hunter like Ging thinks, even when they don’t agree with his methods.
